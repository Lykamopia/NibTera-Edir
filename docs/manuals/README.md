# NibTera Edir — Documentation Library

Welcome to the official documentation set for **NibTera Edir**, a secure, multi-tenant
platform for managing Ethiopian *Edir* community associations — members,
contributions, emergencies, events, assets, and governance — with integrated
digital payments through the **NIB Super App**.

This library is organized into three role-oriented manuals. Read the one that
matches your role; cross-references point you to the others where responsibilities
overlap.

| Manual | Audience | What it covers |
|--------|----------|----------------|
| **[User Manual](./USER_MANUAL.md)** | Edir members and everyday app users | Paying contributions through the NIB Super App, checking balances and history, understanding penalties and coverage, submitting self-service requests, managing your account. |
| **[Edir Administrator Manual](./EDIR_MANUAL.md)** | Edir Administrators, treasurers, secretaries, committee members | Running a single Edir day-to-day: members, payments, approvals, emergencies, events, assets, rules & bylaws, documents, member requests, and Edir settings. |
| **[Platform Administrator Manual](./ADMIN_MANUAL.md)** | Super Administrators and organizational (Head Office / District / Branch) staff | Governing the whole platform: districts, branches, Edir registration and lifecycle, platform users, roles & permissions, associations, oversight, security, and integrations. |

## How to use these manuals

- **Procedures** are written as numbered steps you can follow while using the app.
- **> Notes** highlight helpful context.
- **⚠ Warnings** flag actions that are irreversible or have wide impact.
- **Tables** summarize fields, statuses, permissions, and options.
- A **Glossary** and **FAQ** appear at the end of each manual.

## A note on terminology

An **Edir** (እድር) is a traditional Ethiopian mutual-aid association whose members
contribute regularly and receive support during emergencies such as bereavement.
NibTera Edir digitizes this practice while preserving its governance traditions —
most notably the **maker–checker** principle, where sensitive actions require a
second person to approve.
